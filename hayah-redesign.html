Our Story

A Fierce Passion for God & Global Transformation

At Hayah Ministries International, we are consumed by a relentless pursuit of God's presence and a heart to see individuals, communities, and nations transformed by the power of His love.

Through outreach to streets, prisons, hospitals, and remote communities, we bring hope and redemption to those who need it most — operating from Ottawa, Canada and the Middlebelt of Nigeria.

The Bridge Apostolic Ministerial Forum

The Bridge exists to connect, equip, empower, and deploy ministers of the Gospel across nations — bridging generations, graces, callings, territories, and assignments for the advancement of the Kingdom of God.
